About Myesha

I have spent more than ten years in supplemental insurance, and I still open every conversation the same way. Not with whether you want coverage. With what would actually happen to your household if your income stopped for six months.

In that time I have had that conversation with a lot of people, and the pattern rarely changes. Most have a rough answer and an uncomfortable feeling. Very few have a number. The moment we work the number out together, the conversation changes, because you can finally see the shape of the thing you have been avoiding.

The people I work with are usually not shopping for insurance when we meet. They are working, raising families, paying down a mortgage, and quietly assuming that if something went wrong they would find a way through. Sometimes that is true. More often there is a gap between what they believe their coverage does and what it actually pays, and nobody has ever walked them through it.

What ten years teaches you is that the details decide everything. Not the policy name, but the waiting period, how long a benefit actually lasts, whether it follows you if you change jobs, and what is quietly excluded. That is the part I am useful for.

Sometimes the honest answer is that you are already fine, and I will say so. Where there is a real gap, I will show you what Combined Insurance offers, what it costs, and exactly what it does and does not do. No manufactured urgency, and no recommendation I could not defend to your family.

About the worksheets

The Income Protection Calculator, Family Protection Score and Critical Illness Financial Impact worksheets produce educational estimates based entirely on information you enter. They run in your browser and nothing is transmitted unless you complete and submit a form. They are not a financial needs analysis, not financial or tax advice, not a statement of insurability, and not an application for insurance.

An estimated financial gap is not the same as an amount of insurance you need or should buy. Existing coverage, including any benefit through an employer, is deliberately treated conservatively and separately, because eligibility, waiting periods, benefit duration, tax treatment and policy terms vary and cannot be assessed by a calculator. Results do not promise coverage, eligibility, claim approval, savings or any particular outcome.
